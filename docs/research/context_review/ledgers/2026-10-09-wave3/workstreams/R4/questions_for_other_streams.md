# Questions for other streams / Pass B

- Measurement: Does safe share mean an equal share of current stock, a no-decline regenerative yield, or a recovery allocation? Are units and rounding identical between elicitation and action?
- Methods: Are actions simultaneous? What happens when total requests exceed stock? Is requested extraction distinct from realized payout?
- Prompting: Do expert advice and transparency bundle explicit numbers with normative instructions, authority cues, or inter-agent communication?
- Cognition: Are estimates elicited before actions in a way that changes subsequent actions? Does estimate error predict allocation after controlling for model, treatment, and run?
- Statistics: Are treatment contrasts paired by initial state/seed, and are groups/runs the independent units? How are finite-horizon censoring and collapsed runs handled?
- Institutions: Can agents actually author rules, sanction, exclude, or change payoffs? If not, characterize the experiment as information/advice in a commons rather than full institutional self-governance.
- Interpretation: Which observable hypotheses distinguish calculation errors, coordination failure, conditional response to peers, and compliance? Do not infer intent from safe-share/action disagreement alone.
- Coverage: Can the 2026 GovSim reproduction full text be obtained through an authorized source? Its results remain unverified here.
