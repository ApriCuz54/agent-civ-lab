# R6 expectation memo — frozen Pass A

Created 2026-10-09. Outcome-blind: no repository, run log, implementation, baseline or outcome report has been read. Design supplied by coordinator: LLM naming-game agents choose among labels, interact repeatedly, encounter committed minorities; lexical/position priors, scripted updates and memories vary.

E1. Pairwise coordination should often concentrate label use under local positive feedback. This does not require an LLM: random inventory/pruning and reinforcement models provide strong mechanistic nulls. Failure to exceed these nulls would narrow the contribution to implementation or calibration.
E2. Shared initial preferences can produce agreement without social learning. Empty-history individual choice frequencies, balanced position permutations, no-interaction/no-memory controls and label relabelings are necessary. Initial symmetry alone is insufficient because conditional preferences may become asymmetric under memory.
E3. An imposed committed minority may overturn an existing convention, but there is no universal 25% threshold. Outcome depends on memory, initial state, label strength, interaction topology, payoff and commitment policy. Finite sweeps locate ranges conditional on these settings, not a law.
E4. Equal payoffs do not guarantee equivalent alternatives for a pretrained LLM. Lexical meaning, tokenization and order can bias results. Persistent asymmetries after relabeling would weaken symmetry-breaking language; memory-conditioned asymmetries are a possible mechanism even with neutral first choices.
E5. Scripted updates and retained context may directly encode success reinforcement. Consensus is a population-level emergent outcome in the weak/dynamical sense even if simple rules generate it; it does not establish novel cognition, spontaneous normative commitment, rich culture, institutions or civilization.
E6. Cultural persistence requires transmission to newcomers/replacement generations, resistance after memory/agent turnover, and lineage-specific continuity against independent-prior controls. Stable choices within the same agents and stored histories establish only within-run persistence.
E7. Claims about spontaneous norms should distinguish uncommitted local convention formation from externally installed minority commitments. Minority takeover is imposed perturbation/influence, not spontaneous invention of commitment.
E8. Stronger evidence would report between-run variation, uncertainty, independent population replication, exact interaction denominator and baseline provenance; a single seed/finite horizon or apparent sharp transition is not sufficient.

Disconfirmation: no-interaction or fixed-policy controls matching LLM outcomes; lexical/position effects explaining final labels; scripted reinforcement matching concentration and minority response; disappearance at turnover; inconsistent minority response across labels/models. Positive evidence: excess coordination beyond matched priors, robust conditional-history effects, reproducible interaction-dependent lineage persistence and held-out predictions outperforming simple rules.
