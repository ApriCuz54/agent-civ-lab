Counts of thousands of cells are not thousands of independent model populations. No sampling frame makes the twelve configurations a probability sample of all LLMs. Four Meta labels and multiple within-family configurations weight that family more heavily than singleton families; seven family labels are not seven independent training lineages.

## Identified issues versus suggested improvements

**Identified structure: crossed dependence is treated as nesting.** `analysis/everyday_effects.py:243` creates fresh within-model cluster samples independently for each model/slot. `_mean_unit:104`, `_bal_unit:131`, and `_t4b_unit:207` correctly preserve comparator pairing within a model. Thus the common simplistic criticism that the analysis uses unpaired arm samples would be wrong. However common problems/templates across models are not jointly sampled as common columns. This matters when generalizing over tasks and models together; the direction/magnitude of error cannot be inferred without covariance analysis. [Owen 2007, section 3.2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/0712.1111) motivates crossed resampling. It does not certify a replacement for this small roster.

**Identified assumption: configurations are resampled independently.** `hier_boot` samples model keys, not families. Leave-one-family-out checks exist (`robustness:402`) and are useful sensitivity analyses, but do not repair dependence or yield population coverage. Family-block/shared-template resampling and family-balanced descriptives are suggestions, with explicit caveats about only seven labels and related lineages. Preserve the original preregistered output and label any alternative inference a methods sensitivity analysis.

**Calibration not established: bootstrap sign tails.** `summarize:261` uses twice the smaller fraction of uncentered bootstrap effects on either side of zero, floors at 1/B, and then Holm-adjusts. Such tails can correspond to inversion of percentile intervals in suitable regular problems; they are not automatically invalid merely because bootstrap draws are uncentered. But small model counts, three-seed binary floors, crossings, matching and eligibility selection are not shown to satisfy the needed conditions. No null-size simulation or independent calibration audit is in the reviewed artifacts. The report's phrase “reported adjusted bootstrap tail score” is therefore appropriate; “p=0.002 proves generalization” is not. Four contrasts at the same adjusted floor are not four equal strengths of evidence. Holm handles arbitrary dependence among valid marginal p-values, but cannot fix invalid marginals. Dror et al. [section 3.2 and section 5](https://aclanthology.org/P18-1128.pdf) emphasize paired tests and data-dependence limitations. Suggested next offline analysis: simulate a null retaining shared-template effects, within-family correlation, fallback policy and matching, then compare achieved type-I error; define the target first. No such simulation was run here.

**Identified conditional-null mismatch in L2.** `analysis/link2.py:109` permutes raw everyday metric y while leaving capability z fixed, then recomputes the partial correlation. Under no x–y association conditional on z, y can remain strongly related to z, so arbitrary permutation need not preserve the null joint distribution. [Winkler et al. 2014, nuisance variables and Freedman–Lane algorithm, pp. 386–387](https://wrap.warwick.ac.uk/id/eprint/65670/1/WRAP_1-s2.0-S1053811914000913-main.pdf) explains why nuisance-adjusted permutation needs its own exchangeability argument. A rank-residual permutation or other conditional test is a proposal, not an exact cure: residual exchangeability, small n, family dependence, ties and noisy capability still need checking. Current L2 point associations are descriptive, reported p-values lack a demonstrated conditional-null guarantee. Since no pair qualified, this does not license a positive discovery; it further weakens any inference of absence.

**Identified H-B6 post-selection/dependence.** `_t4match_units:176` computes matching accuracy on sample zero of the evaluated 48 problems; that same sample enters each mixed ensemble. Eligibility changes with observed accuracy, mixed ensembles overlap, and the bootstrap holds matching sets fixed. This omits uncertainty in the selection step and makes anchor outcomes dependent. A clean future benchmark needs disjoint calibration/evaluation problems and joint prediction-matrix resampling; recomputing selection inside resamples can characterize the current selection rule but cannot create a new holdout. This is a defect for a claim of out-of-sample matched-performance ensemble value, not a corruption of the reported selected-suite point contrast.

**Identified configuration confound and limited positive control.** H-B9 changes 500 versus 60 output-token allowance. Four high-invalid configurations are excluded. It demonstrates a configuration penalty in eight eligible models, not the causal effect of reasoning alone. Moreover arithmetic sensitivity does not establish sensitivity of survival, negotiation or reputation endpoints. A single positive control cannot rescue a floor-bound commons contrast.

## Exclusions, fallback and missingness

`analysis/v2data.py:62` excludes a model at experiment-level invalid/call >10%, not necessarily action opportunities; re-asks enlarge the denominator. Independent review reports the same exclusion set under the alternative denominator. There is no identified changed headline eligibility, but the denominator should be named. Expected cells are inferred from the union observed in primary models, so cells missing in every model are invisible to that method; checking against the frozen `cells()` list is a suggested inventory improvement. Models with no directory are absent rather than explicit all-missing entries. A/B completion inventory mitigates this concern but does not make the implementation general.

Fallback random valid actions mix language-model decisions with harness behavior. They are reasonable operational outcomes if deployment uses that policy; they are not pure model preference or comprehension measures. Report eligible-conditioned effects beside invalid/re-ask rates and all-roster operational descriptives. Eligibility selection after observing output format can preferentially remove reasoning configurations; retain it as the preregistered estimand, disclose selection, and do not silently replace it.

Phase C's eight-model analysis was selected by completion speed after interim summaries were viewed, explicitly disclosed in deviation 1. Quota/access is related to provider/model configuration and therefore cannot be assumed ignorable. This is a changed sample, not necessarily outcome-driven fraud. Primary eight and final twelve must be distinguishable. NVIDIA fallback changes serving stack; exclusion robustness addresses one sensitivity, not identical implementations.

## Floors, equivalence, and generalization

H-B5 game_U and placebo are both 0/36 survival. Resampling observed all-zero outcomes can only return zero; [0,0] contains no evidence that unseen episode success probabilities are exactly equal. Transparency's 9/36 means the environment is not impossible, but does not establish sensitivity of the universalization contrast. Survival can coexist with earlier failure-time differences; a new endpoint would be exploratory unless prospectively declared. [Lakens 2017, discussion pp. 359–360](https://pure.tue.nl/ws/portalfiles/portal/80918653/lakeequi2017.pdf) separates failed superiority from equivalence with justified bounds. Existing L4 values are practical thresholds, not automatically a completed equivalence test. H-B3's saved upper bound +6.6 points is smaller than its +10-point L4 target under the original bootstrap, but it is unsafe to market this as calibrated exclusion of practical benefit until uncertainty is validated.

Six selected phrases do not sample a phrase universe; three harmful arms share one policy and template structure. More model seeds cannot establish phrase-general external validity. Reporting the favorable exploratory transparency observation alongside failed repairs is good practice; promoting its new narrative after seeing outcomes is retrospective hypothesis generation, not additional confirmation. The preregistration freezes full-run tests but follows adaptive pilots and v1 knowledge. Pilot-model exclusion is useful; it does not create unseen task families or prompts.

## Compute and operational benchmarks

[AI Agents That Matter, sections 2–5 and Appendix C](https://arxiv.org/html/2407.01502v1) distinguishes accuracy/cost tradeoffs and holdouts appropriate to claimed generality. Equal five calls in T4 do not equal tokens, hidden reasoning, retries, latency or energy. Free-tier price zero is a budget achievement, not zero compute or deployment economics. Report actual resource counts where available; do not use known implausible smoke throughput fields. The study can claim equal primary sample counts and reproducible $0 access, but not resource-optimal ensembles.

[Tau-bench, section 2.2, equation for pass^k, and section 3 limitations](https://arxiv.org/html/2406.12045v1) separates mean success from consistency across repeated semantic tasks and evaluates terminal database state. Lab balanced correctness/survival differs from repeated-run reliability, monetary utility, fairness and authorization safety. Tau-bench is a related operational comparator, not numerically comparable: dynamic simulated users, API tools and policy-conditioned state changes differ from fixed refund responses and scripts. A more realistic benchmark still has simulator limitations; realism is not a binary certificate.

## Social-simulation validity and source version correction

[PIMMUR v4, introduction, section 3 Scope and Applicability, sections 4.2–4.4](https://arxiv.org/html/2509.18052v4) explicitly targets spontaneous human-like emergence and human-proxy claims; engineering behavior under designed prompts is a different objective. It would be a mistake to treat instructed game agents as invalid solely because they fail a human-simulation checklist. Apply it to society/personality/emergence claims; operational causal prompt contrasts need different construct validation. Its redesigned replications bundle changes, so disappearance of a phenomenon is not isolation of one flaw. Important source correction: current v4 is dated 10 September 2026, and supersedes the smaller v1 audit. Do not merge version-dependent counts or model snapshots. The reviewed newer shared-evaluation preprint (Mandujano Reyes 2026, sections 2–6) offers a fixed-model/random-question framing, but its assumptions do not remove family dependence or H-B6 selection; no wholesale method endorsement follows.
