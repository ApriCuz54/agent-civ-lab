# R8 — reasoning and inference configuration

The defensible arithmetic claim is a performance difference between tested prompting/inference configurations. It does not identify an internal reasoning mechanism or its transfer to cooperation or policy adherence. The frozen expectations anticipated exactly this distinction; the repository adds exclusion selection, retry scoring and an SDK cap exception. No outcomes were recomputed.

## Consequential literature and contrary evidence

S1 Snell et al., arXiv:2408.03314v1, §§2–3, §5.3, §6.2, §7: compute allocation depends on difficulty and proposer/verifier quality. The comparison includes search, trained revision and verifier costs; a nominal output cap is not its compute-matched intervention. Hard cases can favor exploration rather than revision. [Full text](https://arxiv.org/html/2408.03314v1).

S2 Muennighoff et al., arXiv:2501.19393v1, §3.1 Budget forcing, §3.2 Metrics, §5.2, Appendix D: controlled early exit appends thinking delimiter and final-answer cue, while minimum thinking suppresses termination and appends Wait. This changes realized thinking and protects an answer channel; merely cutting all output is different. Scaling flattens and runs into control/context limits. Training and data curation accompany the system; no universal token-benefit law. [Full text](https://arxiv.org/html/2501.19393v1).

S3 Lanham et al., arXiv:2307.13702v1, §2.1 sampling, §§2.3–2.6 interventions, §3.2 addition, §5 limitations: early answering, inserted mistakes, filler tokens and paraphrases separate dependence on stated CoT from length or phrasing. Samples use temperature 0.8 and nucleus 0.95; final answer is elicited separately. Dependence varies by task and model. HellaSwag degrades with CoT; easier addition and larger models can be less dependent on CoT. Intervention sensitivity is a diagnostic, not complete latent-process identification. [Full text](https://arxiv.org/html/2307.13702v1).

S4 Turpin et al., arXiv:2305.04388v1, §§2.2,3.1–3.2,4.2,6.3: input hints and answer-order bias change predictions without faithful acknowledgment. Chosen subjective/hard-to-falsify BBH subset, text-davinci-003 and claude-v1.0 limit generality. Explanation plausibility cannot certify causal reasoning or policy compliance. [Full text](https://arxiv.org/html/2305.04388v1).

S5 Huang et al., arXiv:2310.01798v1, §§2,3.1–3.2,4–5: distinguish intrinsic critique from oracle/external correctness feedback. GPT-3.5-0613 and dated GPT-4, default temperature 1, at most two rounds and reduced GPT-4 samples constrain scope. Internal self-correction may degrade reasoning; the null does not rule out trained revision, tool checks or instruction/style correction. [Full text](https://arxiv.org/html/2310.01798v1).

S6 Chen et al., arXiv:2505.05410v1, §§2.1–2.3,3–5,7.2: paired hints and noise-normalized conditional verbalization evaluate limited faithfulness. Claude thinking allowance is 10k and temperature 0. Reasoning models improve relative to non-reasoning comparators yet retain substantial omissions; RL success and reward-hack disclosure dissociate. Hints can be used without CoT, so monitoring failures cannot be generalized to every reasoning-intensive sabotage task. [Full text](https://arxiv.org/html/2505.05410v1).

S7 arXiv:2607.21433v1, §§3–4, introduction contributions: recent single-distilled-model budget sweep distinguishes forced thinking termination from nonconvergence; budgets 256–4096 versus 10000 ceiling. Saturation on simpler benchmarks contradicts uniformly beneficial length. Mechanistic probes at n=200 have underpowered checkpoint comparisons. This preliminary preprint neither establishes a safe minimum for this roster nor explains its short-format exclusions. [Full text](https://arxiv.org/html/2607.21433v1).

Bounded rational metareasoning: Russell/Wefald, Principles of Metareasoning, AI 49 (1991) 361–395, DOI 10.1016/0004-3702(91)90015-C. Decision-theoretic value of computation depends on whether it changes useful external action, less its cost. This is a normative lens, not empirical LLM evidence. Access limitation: journal full text not recovered; CMU scan is one page and CiteSeer fetch failed. No full-journal review claimed. [Publisher](https://doi.org/10.1016/0004-3702(91)90015-C). A Harvard bounded-rationality PDF opened but text was corrupted and screenshot unavailable; excluded from substantive claims.

## Inference boundaries

Keep visible output, internal reasoning, realized generated tokens, allowed token ceiling, parallel samples, verifier/tool compute, latency and provider failures separate. A short generation may be efficient or prematurely stopped; a long generation may be useful or nonconvergent. Selecting valid configurations after a treatment-sensitive formatting failure estimates a selected subset. The historical preregistered estimate should retain its original name and denominator alongside this interpretation, without changing its verdict.

The arithmetic control establishes sensitivity to one quantitative configuration contrast. It cannot validate sensitivity to social cooperation, policy hierarchy, reputation or commons sustainability. Capability can improve strategic self-interest as well as coordination. Policy adherence must be measured against the actual policy and instruction placement; explanations alone do not establish adherence. T1/T5 answer-only contrasts remain exploratory as described in code.
