## Revision to interpretation, not frozen expectations
The neutral task wording implied customer-phrase variation. Actual arms vary the system instructions, while scripted customer pressure remains fixed. The strongest local description is **selected service instructions alter categorical policy-relative decisions under a fixed pressure script**. The H3 wording ambiguity deserves an independent prompt-semantics audit: is discretion expressly subordinate to the cash prohibition, or can later same-role flexibility reasonably qualify it? Report §4 already recognizes this alternative. Label H1/H2/H3 as preregistered rubric categories, not validated universal risk classes.
