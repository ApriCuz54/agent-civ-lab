# Questions for other streams
These questions request design facts before outcome interpretation.

1. R1/design: What are T,R,P,S, discount/continuation, actual horizon and horizon information in each condition? Does a defection end a game?
2. Methods: Are interactions repeated dyads or random matching; what are population size, incumbent/invader composition, selection/update rule and invader fitness definition?
3. Reputation: What exactly is lifetime/recent aggregation (window length, counts versus rates, priors, thresholds, missing data and minimum observations)? Is punishment assessed with recipient context?
4. Identity/security: Are identities immutable; can entrants reset names, impersonate a reporter, alter an authoritative summary, or only append an explicitly untrusted message?
5. Adversarial stream: What is attacker control and the exact forged content? Distinguish false factual evidence, source spoofing and injected instructions.
6. Behavior stream: Are change points scripted or chosen by LLMs; what makes behavior deceptive rather than simply nonstationary?
7. Statistics: How many independent seeds/trials and model versions/settings? What unit is randomized; how are malformed outputs and failed calls handled?
8. Comparison: Are cooperation, invader payoff, social welfare, detection latency and recovery separately available? Which clean controls exist?
