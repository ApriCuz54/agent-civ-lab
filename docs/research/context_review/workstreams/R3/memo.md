## Consequential primary texts
Seven full main texts were accessed, with methods, central results and limitations read where exposed. This is a focused contextual review, not an exhaustive systematic review or independent replication. Supplementary material and every mathematical proof were not exhaustively audited.

- [S1 Axelrod & Hamilton](https://www.csc2.ncsu.edu/faculty/mpsingh/local/Social/f25/wrap/readings/Axelrod%2BHamilton-evolution-cooperation-1981.pdf): Science pp.1393–1395 separates robustness, stability and initial viability. Cooperation under reciprocal punishment depends on continued interaction; unconditional defection can also persist. Tournament success is conditional on entrants. Use as incentive theory, not evidence of LLM psychology.
- [S2 Friedman & Resnick](https://presnick.people.si.umich.edu/papers/identifiers/081199.pdf): manuscript Sections 2–5, especially Proposition 3 (printed p.14), shows the social cost of costless replaceable identities under its random-matching/turnover model. Section 2 footnote 11 explicitly sets reporting reliability outside scope. Identity persistence and truthfulness are separate assumptions.
- [S3 Schmid et al.](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-023-37817-x): Results, Figs.5 and 9, Discussion and Methods model bounded quantitative higher-order reputation under private noisy assessment. Scores buffer errors for selected norms; intermediate resolution matters. Neither lifetime averages nor deliberate message spoofing are directly tested.
- [S4 Akata et al.](https://arxiv.org/html/2305.16867v2): Results/Fig.3 finds persistent retaliation in its GPT-4 setup. Fig.7c reports no detectable SCoT benefit to human PD scores (p=.64); Fig.7f reports a cooperation-rate effect (p=.01). The distinction between reward and cooperation is consequential. Methods uses finite games and prompt-carried histories.
- [S5 Pal et al.](https://academic.oup.com/pnasnexus/article/5/6/pgag210/8705778): Fig.1C and parameter-change sections make inferred strategy robustness contingent on termination assumptions. The finite-time-horizon section finds continued cooperation except Gpt-5's last-round defections; no full backward induction. Discussion notes prompt/label sensitivity and limits of bounded-memory elicitation. This is contrary evidence to a uniform rational-agent prediction.
- [S6 Chupilkin](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2605.03604): Treatments pp.8–9 and Exhibit 1/Prevalence of wars p.10 describe 320 security-dilemma games. Known ten-period horizons yielded conflict in all observed games. A single attack terminates the encounter, so this is adjacent evidence rather than a direct continuing-PD replication. Reasoning-text classification is descriptive, not a causal intervention.
- [S7 Zhu et al.](https://arxiv.org/html/2602.07777v1): Propositions 3.4–3.5 establish existence under incentive and monitoring conditions, not uniqueness. Sections 5.1–5.3 report gossip benefits and reduced cooperation with silent always-defect entrants; Section 5.2/Table21 discusses deceptive self-reports. Five seeds per scenario and equilibrium guidance in main prompts limit generalization. Its untruthful-message tests do not establish resistance to arbitrary authoritative record impersonation.

## What this evidence can establish
Theory specifies when future consequences can sustain cooperation; it does not guarantee agents select the cooperative equilibrium. Empirical studies characterize particular model versions, objectives, prompts, game structures and observation channels. Neither a cooperative trajectory nor plausible strategic language proves an underlying stable strategy. Off-path response elicitation is useful, but the resulting bounded-memory policy is an approximation to deployed history-dependent behavior.

Lifetime/recent tradeoffs and forged-record susceptibility remain transfer hypotheses. The exact reputation update, what recipients see, how identities are bound, and whether the attacker can alter authoritative evidence must be matched before any novelty or contradiction claim. Scripted behavior isolates environment response but does not demonstrate autonomous strategic deception.
