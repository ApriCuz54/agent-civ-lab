# Questions for outcome/design streams

1. Are checked outcomes available for every worker each round, or only the selected one? Is exploration guaranteed?
2. Are history counts and uncertainty shown, or only accuracy percentages? Is recent history measured in global rounds or per-worker observations?
3. Is the worker shift abrupt, gradual, synchronized or worker-specific? Do shifts alter task difficulty or the best worker?
4. Does confidence attach to the current answer or overall worker competence? Are values manipulated independently of correctness/history?
5. Does verification language describe an actual externally checked action, or only a self-assertion? Is verification evidence displayed?
6. Are confidence, length, answer content, identity, position and formatting held constant except for the intended cue?
7. Which selector/worker versions and sampling settings were used? How many independent runs and seeds? Are selection trajectories available?
8. Can cue sensitivity be decomposed into correct versus incorrect following, and shift lag versus steady-state accuracy?
9. What exactly is the frozen baseline artifact/hash? Please provide it only after the expectation freeze recorded above.

These are interpretation questions, not requests for additional experiments or evidence of any observed outcome.
