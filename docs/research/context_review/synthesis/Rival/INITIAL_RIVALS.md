## Central adversarial conclusion

The strongest surviving contribution candidate is a bounded cross-model transfer evaluation with explicit unsuccessful interventions, plus a reusable controlled harness. A broad discovery that rhetoric defeats evidence, information fails to produce cooperation, diversity is ineffective, or AI societies develop culture is not supported. The principal rival is that the observed effects track ordinary prompt/representation changes and tightly constrained task mechanics, while uncertain population inference and measurement ambiguity limit mechanism claims. This rival does not make the descriptive contrasts disappear and does not erase engineering or replication value.

Novelty must be adjudicated against exact treatment, observation and action channels, not titles or the mere existence of related research. Nearby worker/tool precedents already manipulate authority, contamination and reliability signals. They threaten broad novelty, but they do not automatically anticipate the checked temporal-performance panel and scripted deterioration in T2. Conversely, adding more model labels or using free endpoints is useful scope/infrastructure work without automatically being a new behavioral theory.

## Rival hypotheses and discriminators

### RV1: T2 notes shift attention or add a conflicting evidence source, rather than reveal a general confidence pathology

The baseline records harm after adding all worker notes while retaining checked lifetime history. In `civlab/everyday/t2_trust.py:41` panel preserves lifetime counts/percentages, while `:65` answer_lines adds 90%, 60%, and the combined `verified, 99% confident` note for the scripted deteriorating worker. This is an entire note bundle. Misleading verification, numeric confidence, extra length, answer-local salience and conflict resolution are inseparable. Calling it an identified confidence mechanism, autonomous worker deception, or human automation bias exceeds the contrast.

Strong rival: agents treat the local, ostensibly item-specific assertion as a new signal while historical accuracy estimates a past-regime mixture. That inference would be reasonable if assertions were calibrated; here the harness intentionally makes one persistent assertion misleading after the shift. Alternative rival: added text distracts from the checked panel even without semantic trust. Either can produce the same accuracy loss. The favorable calibrated-confidence literature in R1 is genuine contrary evidence to universal rejection of self-report.

Falsification/discrimination requires fixed-content, equal-length numeric-only, verification-only, neutral-note and calibrated-item-confidence conditions with authority/source bindings held constant; preserve both clean utility and harmful susceptibility. These are future design targets, not authorized runs or a claim that existing data can identify them. Existing read-only verification can establish prompt identity, role/letter randomization, post-shift trajectories and fallback contribution.

Closest sources: R1 memo S1 Tian §§2–3/Tables1–5, S3 Yang §§3.2/5.4; Coordinator gap extraction, TrustFork 2609.32635v1 §§3.1–4/5.2–5.6/Appendix F, Agents’ Overreliance 2609.05587v2 §§3–5. ### RV5: higher posted pricing is instruction following or inefficient learning, not economic collusion

`civlab/games/pricing.py` round_profits, SYSTEM and INTERVENTIONS define homogeneous integer Bertrand prices with full history, and avoidance wording explicitly invokes everyone’s profits. High average posted-price index can arise from direct objective framing, poor undercut reasoning or nominal price averaging. A (10,30) price pair yields a high average index while consumers transact at10 and firm profit is zero; (30,30) yields a still higher index and no sales. Hence index is not monotonic in welfare or profit. The integer game admits both (10,10) and (11,11) as stage equilibria, so cost10 is not a unique discrete equilibrium benchmark.

Discriminator: realized transaction price/profits and purposeful deviation/punishment responses against the inferred policy, with economic baseline and horizon explicit. Saved four-seed threshold counts remain descriptive wording sensitivity. They do not certify stable collusive strategy, memory’s causal role or welfare effects. R5 memo S1 Fish 2404.00806v2 §§2–5/App.B,D,E; S2 Calvano April2019 working paper §§4.4–5.5 pp19–29 are mechanism comparators, not exact replications. The code qualification is algebraic, not new result analysis.

## Methods concerns with their proper scope

Paired arms are preserved in `_mean_unit:104`, `_bal_unit:131`, `_t4b_unit:207`; a criticism of universally unpaired samples is false. `hier_boot:243` treats configurations independently and resamples shared templates separately by configuration. That is consequential for claims about new models and new tasks jointly, not automatic erasure of fixed-suite means. Leave-one-family-out checks are sensitivity analyses, not guaranteed population coverage. Training-family labels are not independent sampled lineages.

`summary:261` bootstrap sign tails have unestablished calibration in this small selected design. Being uncentered alone does not prove invalidity. Holm addresses multiplicity given valid marginal p-values; it cannot certify marginal calibration. Preserve historical adjusted tail scores and practical thresholds, with uncertainty caveats. An all-zero survivor bootstrap cannot exclude meaningful unseen success-probability differences. Completion-selected Phase C eight-model primary and incomplete twelve-model snapshot remain distinct.

Fallback decisions are harness outputs, and invalid-per-call denominators include retry mechanics; this matters for pure preferences/comprehension and eligibility. It is not a defect in evaluating the documented operational fallback policy. Pre-registration after pilots constrains full-run analytical flexibility without making task/phrase construction an independent holdout. These are R9 memo’s implemented-estimands, exclusions, floors and inference sections, not freshly verified numerical findings.
