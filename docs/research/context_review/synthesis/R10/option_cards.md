## Stop criteria for further contribution hunting

If nearest-paper verification confirms that only the task/roster assembly differs, prefer A/C or E. If a candidate claim requires mechanism/held-out/compute controls not present, classify unresolved and park rather than reconstructing a novelty story. Search failure never establishes priority. Additional searching is justified only by a specified candidate claim and a discriminating comparator.
