# What Agent Civ Lab can credibly become

**Decision brief for Aditya · 9 October 2026 · Context review delivered; scoped source verification complete.**

**Recommendation:** finish a narrow experimental-engineering report and measurement appendix using the existing work. If you want a further research study, select one specific unanswered mechanism afterward. The present package does not establish a new theory of agent behavior or a general law about game-theory transfer. That does not make the work valueless: it demonstrates a substantial evaluation pipeline and preserves informative failures. This recommendation is a contribution assessment, not a publication or experiment authorization.

## 1. What changed after adding context

The original numbers are interesting, but their meaning depends on what agents were told, what actions they could take, how the harness scored them, and which comparison the experiment actually made. The review covered nine areas: worker evidence, instruction conflicts, repeated games, commons, markets, conventions, ensembles, reasoning configuration, and evaluation methods. Independent constructive and rival interpretations were cross-examined against the nearest-work map. Executive source/definition checks constrain the statements retained here; detailed verification is in [the signoff](../verification/FINAL_SIGNOFF.md).

The most consequential change is from broad behavioral descriptions to specific operational claims. “Agents trust rhetoric over evidence” becomes a result about adding one particular worker-note bundle while checked history remains visible. “Agents know the limit and take too much” becomes disagreement between stated estimates and allocation under an ambiguous benchmark. “Collective bias emerges” becomes initial preference concentration in the cross-model naming test. These narrower statements describe what was measured and leave competing explanations visible.

We should also avoid letting criticism erase observations. Same-bank matching and related model families restrict generalization; they do not make every raw contrast meaningless. Overlap does not invalidate every estimator. Uncentered bootstrap draws alone do not prove invalid hypothesis testing. This review identifies unresolved calibration and design assumptions rather than claiming that a replacement analysis has already shown different effects.

## 2. The findings worth keeping

### Worker evidence is the clearest optional focal case

The saved audit reports a **9.9 percentage-point post-shift accuracy loss across twelve eligible configurations** when worker confidence notes were added to retained cumulative checked history. The worker deterioration was scripted. The notes combined numbers, confidence, and claimed verification; the experiment did not isolate their components. Recent history had a smaller uncertain advantage. These are finite-suite descriptive estimates from the existing audit, not new inference in this review. [Local audit](../../../report/evidence_audit.md) and [E02–E03 checks](../verification/Executive_A/checks.md).

This makes a good engineering case about the interaction between checked records and additional assertions. It does not establish that confidence is generally useless, that the model suffers human automation bias, or that workers autonomously deceive. Favorable confidence-calibration results are genuine counterexamples to a universal interpretation. [Just Ask for Calibration](https://aclanthology.org/2023.emnlp-main.330.pdf).

Closest recent work also narrows novelty. TrustFork manipulates displayed identity and operational authority in executable tasks; Agents’ Overreliance corrupts tool returns and evaluates adoption under exposure. Our temporal checked-history selector is different, but difference alone does not establish priority or an important extension. The promising unanswered question is whether misleading cues displace verified temporal evidence independently of length, salience and actual item information. [TrustFork v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.32635v1), [Overreliance v2](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.05587v2).

### Policy language changes the specification, as well as behavior

The refund study adds selected phrases to the **same system message** as the numeric policy. It scores categorical decisions against a fixed rubric. Flexibility wording may authorize an exception that the rubric penalizes, while other phrases behave differently. The important finding is sensitivity of policy-relative decisions to selected application-side instructions. Generic customer prompt-injection, instruction-hierarchy failure, and sycophancy are not isolated mechanisms. [E04 checks](../verification/Executive_A/checks.md).

This can support a useful specification/measurement case. A more convincing future study would explicitly distinguish strict rules from delegated exception authority, vary customer pressure separately, and evaluate held-out policies and phrases. Current data cannot supply those controls retrospectively. Instruction-hierarchy and interactive policy benchmarks provide comparison disciplines rather than numerical forecasts for this task. [Instruction Hierarchy](https://arxiv.org/html/2404.13208v1), [tau-bench](https://arxiv.org/html/2406.12045v1).

### Commons results need institutional and metric context

In the saved shared-budget audit, control, universalization and placebo each survived **0/36** episodes; calculated transparency survived **9/36**. The latter bundled arithmetic with history. A severe floor makes zero differences uninformative about equivalence or universal failure of advice. The local intervention and environment differ from GovSim. Studies with executable voted caps, sanctions, redistribution and membership changes test another intervention entirely. [E05–E06 checks](../verification/Executive_A/checks.md), [governance verification](../verification/Executive_B/checks.md), [GovSim](https://arxiv.org/html/2404.16698v3).

Phase C’s SAFE scoring expresses restoration to capacity, while literal “no reduction in future availability” can mean a different limit below capacity. Equal sharing is also a researcher benchmark rather than an automatically binding duty. Stated estimates and requests therefore cannot establish comprehension, deliberate selfishness or intent. The preregistration P−50 versus implemented P−48 discrepancy remains disclosed. The incomplete cloud addendum remains incomplete in the frozen report snapshot. These qualifications belong in any paper using the result.

### Coordination and computation remain conditional

The ensemble comparison concerns eligible, selected configurations on a reused problem bank with overlapping memberships. It does not establish general diversity failure or equal realized compute. The brief-working versus answer-only comparison also changes format and requested output allowance; the Claude route omits the per-call cap. It does not isolate latent reasoning or prove truncation caused the accuracy difference. Recent ensemble studies themselves require attention to generation counts, temperature and token budgets. [E07–E08 checks](../verification/Executive_B/checks.md), [Beyond Symmetric Agents](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.35875v1).

### Social and economic labels need restrained interpretation

The cross-model naming test uses independent empty-history choices. Initial concentration is an existing measurement class, not newly demonstrated emergence. Separate historical interaction and minority experiments require their own controls; the external critique/reply dispute does not prove local training-data leakage. [E09 checks](../verification/Executive_B/checks.md).

Pricing measures average posted-price indices, not transaction prices, profit, welfare or punishment. The implemented integer game has an additional symmetric stage equilibrium, so the marginal-cost reference is not unique. Reputation and repeated-game trajectories describe reactions to specified histories and opponents, not stable preferences or an evolutionarily stable strategy. [E10–E11 checks](../verification/Executive_C/checks.md).

## 3. What the numbers cannot yet support

Thousands of cells do not supply thousands of independent model populations. The roster is fixed; model families, shared templates, eligibility and selected ensembles complicate uncertainty. The historical confidence intervals and adjusted tail scores retain their original caveats. We have not performed a new calibration simulation or inferred an achieved error rate.

No game–task pair qualified under the original rule. That is a failed positive result, not evidence that transfer is impossible. The implementation permutes raw outcomes while holding capability fixed; an appropriate conditional-null guarantee has not been established. The primary nuisance/exchangeability passage is now independently verified, but a valid replacement for this design remains unresolved. [E12 check and corrected locator](../verification/Executive_C/checks.md).

There is also no certified exhaustive literature coverage or unique full-text count. The preserved inventory contains **83 source records, 91 claim records and 88 search records**. Versions, duplicate lanes, related authors/datasets, English/open-access bias and unavailable texts restrict interpretation. Independent executive checks and an eight-row sample support selected passages; they do not independently reproduce all papers, artifacts or local statistical results.

## 4. Choosing a project direction

For a portfolio, the most defensible current product is **a finite-roster transfer evaluation with a measurement appendix**: exact tasks and interventions, raw-evidence provenance, failed repairs, corrected construct definitions, and restricted conclusions. A reader can assess concrete implementation and experimental judgment. Full independent artifact execution remains unfinished, so the package should be described as inspectable rather than certified reproducible. A venue-worthy novelty claim remains provisional.

If you want a research extension, worker-evidence cue isolation is the strongest optional focus because the current bundle effect is clear and the missing control is specific. Objective-conflict evaluation, governance interventions, and held-out budget-aware ensembles are genuinely different alternatives; their merit depends on the question you want to answer, not their ability to rescue the original narrative. [Detailed option cards](RESEARCH_OPTIONS.md).

My suggested order is: prepare the bounded report and clean evidence presentation; choose at most one further question; specify its controls, holdouts, actual budgets and null contribution before any new calls. A null should still yield an interpretable benchmark or mechanism boundary. Stop the proposed extension if its nearest precedents already answer the same question or essential controls exceed the available resources. No experiment follows automatically from this recommendation.

Archiving the current package and stopping is a legitimate option. Do not spend another round merely to chase a publishable positive result. Conversely, a careful negative transfer report can have engineering value even if it cannot sustain a novel behavioral theory.
