## What remains constructive without a general reliability story

A bounded transfer evaluation remains a coherent present contribution. The protocol asks a practical question and includes placebo/expert comparisons and unsuccessful repairs. Its reporting value is preserving the exact tested intervention, task mechanics and failed superiority criterion, rather than claiming all game-theoretic lessons fail. This survives Rival’s central adversarial conclusion and R10 optionA even if none of the three initial interpretive frames becomes a new theory. It can be useful as scope extension, conceptual replication and reproducible engineering assembly; these labels require exact nearest-work matching and artifact certification before promotion.

The objective/environment correspondence frame is viable as a measurement note (R10 optionC), because implementation qualifiers genuinely change what the outcomes mean while leaving scores intact. It need not allege that ambiguity caused every effect. The novel artifact, if any, would be an explicit, reusable mapping from question → prompted objective → action channel → metric → warranted inference. Merely collecting known caveats is insufficient for a novelty claim; independently executable checks and a discriminating comparator would establish additional value. PIMMUR human-proxy criteria should apply to emergence/culture claims, not invalidate instructed engineering evaluation.

The worker-interface case remains the clearest narrow optional emphasis (R10 optionB). Its possible distinctiveness is checked temporal worker performance plus deliberately misleading local notes after drift, with factual-answer selection as endpoint. That is different from granting execution authority to a branded endpoint or contamination adoption conditioned on exposure. Difference in design is not automatically scientific novelty; it becomes a useful extension only if it tests a specific unanswered question rather than renaming an established effect.

Resource/prior work is primarily a prospective direction, not a present mechanism contribution. Held-out matching, deployable roster rules and resource budgets would answer a different engineering question; naming controls would answer a different emergence question. They should not be joined merely to support a social-intelligence narrative. R10 optionD is correctly parked. Narrow completed report plus measurement appendix, or internal archive/stop, requires no invention of new positive findings.
