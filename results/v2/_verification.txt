5. H-B5  T3 game_U - placebo, week-8 survival                                            MATCH (exactly 0)
   The effect is 0.000 for all 12 models. This is a floor effect: across all models and seeds, survival is
   0/36 under game_U, 0/36 under placebo and 0/36 under control. Every run in both arms locked the pool before
   the end of week 8, so the binary metric cannot detect any difference. Other arms do vary (game_T 9/36,
   expert 1/36), so the metric is not stuck at zero everywhere.
   Descriptive only, not a registered test: game_U does delay the collapse. Mean lock week is 2.25 under game_U
   vs 1.47 under placebo (36 runs each). The binary endpoint does not capture this.
